How to undersand simple aspect?

First we need to know that the simple aspect has two tipes of sentenses:
First type - Action sentence or Verb sentence.
Second type - Non-verb sentences or "To be" sentence.
Why do we name these two types like that?
Action sentence or Verb sentence stands for sentences which contain any verb. If there is a verb so the sentence automatically discribes a physical(walk) or mental(know) action.
Non-verb sentence or "To be" sentence stands for sentences where is no verb so no actoin. These sentences describe a state of being(sad or happy), a characteristic(tall or small), a profesion(a doctor or a student), or a location(I am at home).
So here are the formulas.
Verb sentence: Subject+Verb
"To be" sentence: Subject+To be+complement.

Next what we need to know is there is three tenses: Present, Past and Future and each has Simple aspect.
So we have Present Simple, Past Simple and Future Simple, and each has two tipes of sentences.
So, what do we have now?
Present Simple has two types of sentences (Verb sentence and "To be" sentence)
Past simple has two types of sentences (Verb sentence and "To be" sentence)
Future Simple has two types of sentences (Verb sentence and "To be" sentence)
Let's separate them into two groups by the types and make it simple. Let's start from "To be" sentences for Present Simple, Past Simple and Future Simple. They share only the formula (Subject+To be+complement) but if they have one formula, how do we know what tense it is? and there is somthing what really helps. It is different "To be" forms for each tense.

Let's have a look at these "To be" forms!
"To be" Forms for Present tense are: am, is, are.
"To be" Forms for Past tense are: was, were.
"To be" Form for Future tense is: will be.
So when we need to make Present tense we put enstead of "To be": am, is or are.
When we need to make Past tense we put enstead of "To be": was, were.
When we need to make Future tense we put enstead of "To be": will be.
Next we need to know what "To be" forms use dipends of? And this is very easy!
It depends of Subdjects (I, you, we, they, he, she, it).
