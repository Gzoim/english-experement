How to understand the Simple Aspect?

First, we need to know that the Simple aspect has two types of sentences:

Verb sentence (Action sentence)

Action sentence (or Verb sentence) stands for sentences which contain a verb.
If there is a verb, the sentence automatically describes a physical (e.g., walk) or mental (e.g., know) action.
Formula: Subject + Verb

Non-verb sentence ("To be" sentence)

Non-verb sentence (or "To be" sentence) stands for sentences where there is no verb, so there is no action.
These sentences just describe information about someone or something (e.g., small, tall, happy, sad, cold...).
Formula: Subject + To be + Complement

Next, what we need to know is that there are three tenses: Present, Past and Future, and each has a Simple aspect. So we have Present Simple, Past Simple and Future Simple, and each has two types of sentences.

Let's separate them into two groups by the type and start from "To be" sentences. Present, Past and Future non-verb sentence in Simple aspect share the exact same formula:

Non-verb sentence Formula
Subject + to be + Complement
But if they have the same formula, how do we know what tense it is? There is something that really helps us: it is different "To be" forms for each tense. Look for them on the right side of page, in the Help Verbs section.
How does it work? Let's have a look at these "To be" forms!

Present
am / is / are
Past
was / were
Future
will be

"To be" Forms for Present tense are: am, is, are.
"To be" Forms for Past tense are: was, were.
"To be" Form for Future tense is: will be.
So when we need to make a Present tense sentence, we use am, is or are instead of "To be".
When we need to make a Past tense we use was, were instead of "To be".
When we need to make a Future tense we use will be instead of "To be".
Next, we need to know is that the "To be" forms use depends on Subdject (I, you, we, they, he, she, it).

Present "To be" forms:
use am for I (I am happy)
use is for he, she, it (She is happy)
use are for you, we, they (We are happy)

Past "To be" forms:
use was for I, he, she, it (I was happy)
use were for you, we, they (We were happy)

Future "To be" form:
use will be for I, he, she, it, you, we, they (I will be happy)

So again, the formula for Simple acpect of "To be" sentences is Subject + To be + Complement.
We can choose any subject we need, than add the correct "To be" form for that subject. If we want to say "She", then the "To be" form can be: is for present, was for past or will be for future.
What is a complement? A complement describes any information about the subdject.
If to dig deep into the complement we can see the end of "To be" sentences! The coomplement is evrything that "To be" sentences Present, Past and Future can describe. There is nothing more in this world can be discribed in "To be" sentences -- only what the complement includes!

All Types of Subdject Complements:
Profession - It tells us subject's occupation, job or career. (He is a techer. Developer, Designer, Copywriter, Blogger...)
Status - It is the person's life role in a society or in a family. (She is a mother. Student, Friend, Single, Member, Winner...)
Characteristic - It describes a permanent quality or personalyty trait that doesn't change easily. Describer what this object is by its nature. (The car is fast. Tall, Young, Smart, Red, Flat...)
State - It describes a temporary physical or emotional feelings right now. It can be changed quickly. (I am tired. Hungry, Happy, Confused, Ready, Broken, Cold, Busy...)
Identity - It points at someone or something to tell us exactly who or what they are. (It is John. That is Ukraine, Kyiv, Me, The boss, The key...)
Prossession - It tells us who owns an object. (This key is mine. The house is theirs, Yours, His, Hers, Ours, John's, Mary's...)
Age - It tells us how many years someone or something has existed. (He is twenty. The building is very old, New, Ancient...)
Time - It tells us the hour, the part of the day, the day of the week. (It is five o'clock. Today is Monday, Summer, Late, Time to go...)
Number - It shows any mathematical number, score or digit. (The score is three-to-one. My favorite number is ate, The first, The last...)
Location - It tells us exectly where a person or an object is in space or where it is from. (We are at home. The cat is under the table, From Ukraine, In the world, Online...)
