Where to begin?

The left side of page containce navigation of all tenses.
In English, there are three tenses Present, Past, Future, and four aspects for each of them Simple, Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous.

Each tense has its own formula and shares some similarities by their aspects, but every single one is unique. If you learn how to make sentense just for one aspect, you already know how to do it for the other tenses with the same aspect.

For example, if we know the structure of Present Simple, we automatically know Past Simple and Future Simple.
So, I think it's great idea to go by asppects rather than by tenses. Also I sugest to start from continuius aspect, it's the moust easiest one to undestand.
